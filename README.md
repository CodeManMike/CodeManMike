## What I'm building

### [RecoveryOS](https://recoveryos.org)

A private, local-first recovery app for Android. It isn't a 12-step program or a day counter. It's a daily practice: body-first tools to regulate when a craving hits, a craving log that shows your patterns without judgement, a daily check-in across seven areas of recovery, and a slip-aware clock that doesn't throw you back to day zero. Your data stays on your phone.

I'm in recovery myself. I built RecoveryOS for me first, and I use it every day. It's in beta testing now, and you can sign up at [recoveryos.org](https://recoveryos.org).

Built with React, Vite and Capacitor, with Firebase for the site and sign-ups.

### [The Tube Desktop](https://github.com/CodeManMike/the-tube-desktop)

YouTube as a proper Windows app. A queue that stays put while you browse, a floating picture-in-picture window, SponsorBlock, a tray icon and media hotkeys, with your own account, 4K and HDR. It's free. [Download the latest release](https://github.com/CodeManMike/the-tube-desktop/releases/latest).

Built with Electron and TypeScript, on castLabs' Widevine build.
